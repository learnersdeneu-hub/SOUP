# About SOUP

SOUP (Students Online University Portal) helps international students get from "I don't know where to apply" to "I've landed, enrolled and settled in". It brings together an AI admissions counsellor, experienced human counsellors and the practical services students need along the way: insurance, accommodation and scholarship guidance.

We built SOUP because the study-abroad process is broken for most families. A student applying to eight universities in three countries types the same name, passport number and grades into eight different portals. They collect the same documents again and again, and they rely on scattered blog posts, agents with conflicting incentives, or guesswork. SOUP replaces that with **one profile, one plan and one place to track everything.**

## Where we come from

SOUP did not start as an app. It grew out of **Learners Den**, a counselling practice that has guided students through university applications by hand since 2020. That work showed us where students actually get stuck, such as missing documents, wrong deadlines, inconsistent forms and unclear visa steps. Each version of the platform has been tested with real students:

- **Learners Den:** one-to-one counselling and application support.
- **learnersden.eu:** our first online platform for students and applications.
- **SOUP Beta:** student accounts, documents and applications in one place.
- **SOUP v2:** Noodles, our AI counsellor, plus a university network and live dashboards.
- **SOUP Companion:** our newest layer, which takes a student's SOUP profile onto real application forms.

## What SOUP does

### Noodles, the AI admissions counsellor

Noodles is the first thing most students meet on SOUP. Students describe their grades, budget, preferred countries and goals in plain language, and Noodles helps them:

- understand which universities and programmes realistically fit;
- compare countries, costs and entry routes;
- build a **University Application Plan** that can be downloaded as a PDF;
- work out what documents and steps come next.

Noodles researches current information rather than relying on old lists. Where a requirement changes often, such as a fee, deadline or visa rule, it points students to the official source.

### Human counsellors

AI is good at structure and speed. It is not a substitute for judgement on a borderline application or a complicated family situation. Students on our paid plans get access to SOUP counsellors, and SOUP Concierge adds weekly video sessions and senior counsellor oversight of the whole case.

### University and programme discovery

Students can search SOUP's university and programme catalogue and shortlist up to three universities in **My Colleges**, or up to ten with an upgrade. SOUP then merges the shortlisted universities' requirements into a single checklist.

We separate **SOUP partner universities** from **independent recommendations**. Suitable partners may be shown first, but students always see public and non-partner options that fit them too. We never show "admission chance" percentages. No honest platform can promise those.

### Application preparation

SOUP's application workspace follows the structure students meet on real portals:

1. Profile and personal details
2. Education background
3. Extracurricular activities and recommendations
4. Testing (English tests, SAT/ACT, GRE/GMAT)
5. Funding

Students enter their information once. They can invite recommenders by email, draft a base personal statement or motivation letter, and keep a secure **document vault** for their passport, transcripts and test reports, with expiry reminders.

### SOUP Companion

SOUP Companion is our Windows desktop app for filling in application forms. Students sign in, open an application website inside Companion, and Companion recognises the form's fields. It fills in what it can from their SOUP profile, attaches matching documents from their vault, and flags anything missing or inconsistent. If it is unsure which document to use, it asks. **The student always reviews and submits the application themselves.**

A browser-based, reference-only version is available on paid plans for students who can't install the desktop app.

## What we will not do

Trust matters more than speed in admissions, so we hold ourselves to a few rules:

- **No guaranteed admissions or visas.** Universities and governments make those decisions, not SOUP.
- **No invented facts.** Where we publish fees, deadlines or requirements, we link to the official source and show when we last checked it.
- **No hidden steering.** Commercial partnerships never decide which universities are "right" for a student.
- **Your data is yours.** Documents are stored privately, and Companion only acts on pages the student opens and asks it to fill.
