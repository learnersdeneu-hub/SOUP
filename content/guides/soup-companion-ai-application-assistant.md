# SOUP Companion: The AI Application Assistant for University Applications

Ask any student who has applied abroad what the worst part was, and few will say the essay. They'll describe **the forms**: the same name, passport number, school history, grades and parents' details, typed again and again into portal after portal. Then come the uploads, the re-uploads, the "invalid format" errors, and the fear of a typo sitting in an application that's already been sent.

**SOUP Companion** was built to take that work off students, and to make every application better before it's submitted. This guide explains what Companion is, exactly what it does, the problem it solves, and why we believe it's one of the most important developments in admissions technology right now.

> **Last reviewed:** 18 November 2026. Companion is updated often; this guide describes the current release.

![How SOUP Companion works: one SOUP profile feeds Companion, which scans, fills, attaches and reviews applications on UCAS, the Common App, university portals and PDF forms. The student always submits.](/images/guides/companion-how-it-works.svg)

## What is SOUP Companion?

SOUP Companion is a **Windows desktop app** that connects to your SOUP account. It works like a browser, with tabs and an address bar, but with the SOUP assistant panel beside every page you open.

When you open a university application, Companion:

1. **Scans the page** and recognises what each field is asking for.
2. **Fills the fields** from your SOUP profile, including text boxes, dropdowns, radio buttons and checkboxes, then **checks each fill** to make sure it actually stuck.
3. **Attaches your documents** from your SOUP vault into upload fields, such as your passport, transcripts and English test report. If two files could fit, it **asks you which one** instead of guessing.
4. **Reviews the whole application** with AI before you submit, the way an experienced counsellor would in the final minutes before the deadline.

Everything it does appears in a live activity terminal beside the page, so you can see exactly what it scanned, filled, attached and flagged.

**It never submits an application for you.** You review the application and press submit yourself.

## The problem: applying abroad means typing the same answers into many forms

The scale of the repetition is easy to underestimate.

![Chart: in the 2025–26 Common App season, 1.53 million applicants submitted 10.8 million applications, an average of 7.06 each. UNESCO counts about 7.3 million internationally mobile students in 2023.](/images/guides/companion-application-repetition.svg)

- On the **Common App** alone, **1.53 million** first-year applicants submitted **10.8 million** applications in the 2025–26 season, an average of **7.06 applications each**.
- **UNESCO** counts roughly **7.3 million internationally mobile students** worldwide in 2023, a number that has more than tripled since 2000.
- International applicants rarely stay inside one system. A student from Lahore, Dubai or Riyadh might use **UCAS** for the UK, the **Common App** for the US, **Studielink** and university portals in the Netherlands, **Universitaly** for Italy, the **Study in Greece** platform, and then separate **visa forms** after that.

Common App and UCAS each reduce repetition **inside their own system**. Nothing reduced it **across** systems, and that's where international students spend most of their time and make most of their mistakes.

## What Companion does, feature by feature

### 1. One profile, filled everywhere

You build your SOUP profile once: personal details, passport, education history, grades, test scores, activities, references and essays. Companion uses that single source of truth on every form, so your passport number is identical on every application.

### 2. It reads the form, not just the field names

Application portals are built in very different ways. Companion recognises fields by their labels, names and surrounding text, using a dictionary designed specifically for admissions forms. It handles radio buttons and custom dropdowns that ordinary browser autofill ignores, and it **verifies every value after filling**.

### 3. Documents attached automatically

Upload fields are classified the same way: passport, transcript, English test, CV, bank statement and so on. Companion attaches the matching file from your vault. When it's ambiguous, for example if you have both an IELTS and a TOEFL report, it **pauses and asks**.

### 4. An AI review before you submit

Once a form is filled, Companion runs an **AI application review** built from exactly what's on that form: every field label, the value about to be submitted, and which documents were and weren't attached. It reports:

- **Overall readiness:** Strong, Needs work or Not ready.
- **Strengths** worth keeping.
- **Gaps**, with a specific suggestion for each.
- **Credibility notes**: answers that look incomplete or implausible, or that **don't match what your SOUP record says**.

Readiness isn't decided by AI opinion alone. During a run, Companion also checks SOUP's own **application checklist** for that university, so an application with required items still outstanding can't be marked ready.

### 5. Several applications in one sitting

![Diagram: a multi-application run. Pick applications from My Colleges, open each official form, fill each page and click Next, get an AI review and checklist check, then you approve and submit before Companion moves to the next. Companion pauses for CAPTCHAs, one-time codes, passwords, unknown questions and pages that aren't forms.](/images/guides/companion-multi-application-run.svg)

With a **multi-application run**, you pick applications from **My Colleges** and Companion works through them one after another:

- It opens each application's **official form**. Where SOUP doesn't already have the link, it looks for the university's own official application page.
- It fills each page and **clicks Next through multi-page forms**, but only once every required field on the current page is complete. It never guesses past an incomplete page.
- It **pauses and hands control back to you** for anything that needs a human: a CAPTCHA, a one-time code, a password, a question SOUP has no answer for, or a page that isn't an application form.
- After the review, **you approve** the application. Approval updates your SOUP record only. It never presses the university's submit button.

### 6. It learns your answers

Every portal asks a few questions no other portal asks. When you answer one that SOUP doesn't recognise, Companion saves your answer. **The next time any application asks the same question, it's already answered.**

### 7. PDF forms too

Many visa forms and some universities still use fillable PDFs. Signed-in students can upload a fillable PDF on the web. SOUP fills it from the same profile, lets them review every value, attaches the supporting documents, and returns **one complete, ready-to-submit file**, with the same AI review.

### 8. Always up to date

The desktop app **updates itself in the background**, so students always have the latest field recognition and fixes.

## Why Companion is a genuine innovation in admissions

Edtech has plenty of tools that help with one piece of the application: a search engine, an essay checker, a deadline tracker. Here's how Companion compares with the main alternatives a student has today.

| Approach | Works across different portals? | Fills documents? | Reviews the application? | Student stays in control? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common App / UCAS | Only inside their own system | Within their system | No | Yes |
| Browser autofill | Name, email and address only | No | No | Yes |
| Agent or consultant applies for you | Yes | Yes, by hand | Depends on the agent | **You hand over your logins** |
| AI writing tools | No | No | Essays only | Yes |
| **SOUP Companion** | **Yes, on the forms you open** | **Yes, from your vault** | **Yes, the whole form against your record** | **Yes: it never submits** |

Three things make Companion different:

1. **It works where the work actually is.** Instead of asking universities to join another platform, Companion works on the forms that already exist: UCAS, the Common App, university portals and PDFs.
2. **It combines automation with judgement.** Filling saves time; the review catches the mistakes and weak answers that cost offers. Few tools do both, and fewer check the application against the student's own verified record.
3. **It's built around consent.** Companion pauses whenever a human decision is needed, never guesses forward, and never submits. That's what makes it safe to use on the most important forms a student will fill in.

## Honest limits

We'd rather you trust Companion than overestimate it:

- **It's a Windows app.** On a phone, or a computer where you can't install software, SOUP Scholar and SOUP Concierge include a web Companion: a reference view you can copy answers and documents from. It doesn't fill forms automatically.
- **It won't recognise every field on every portal.** Anything it can't fill is left for you and flagged. **Check every page before you submit.**
- **Some forms can't be reached automatically**, such as forms inside certain embedded frames.
- **Windows may show an "unrecognised publisher" warning** the first time you install it.
- **It doesn't write your essays.** Personal statements and supplementary essays should be your own work.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is SOUP Companion?

SOUP Companion is a Windows desktop app that uses your SOUP profile to fill university application forms, attach your documents and review each application with AI before you submit it yourself.

### Does SOUP Companion submit applications for me?

No. Companion fills and checks forms, but it never presses submit. You review every application and submit it yourself.

### Does it work with UCAS and the Common App?

Companion works on application pages you open inside it, including UCAS, the Common App and university portals. Field recognition covers most common application fields, and anything it can't fill is flagged for you.

### How does the AI application review work?

After filling, Companion sends the form's fields, the values about to be submitted and the attached documents to an AI review. The review reports readiness, strengths, gaps with suggestions, and credibility concerns, including answers that don't match your SOUP record.

### Is SOUP Companion free?

The Windows desktop app and the PDF form filler are available to signed-in SOUP students. The no-install web Companion is included with SOUP Scholar and SOUP Concierge. Current plans are on the [Plans page](/premium).
