# Top 10 Fully Funded Scholarships in Europe for International Students

"Fully funded" is one of the most overused phrases in study-abroad marketing. Many scholarships advertised that way cover tuition but leave you to find €12,000 a year for rent and food, or pay a stipend while you still owe fees.

This list only includes scholarships that **cover or waive tuition and also pay a living allowance**. For each one, we show **who can apply** (with a note for **Pakistani and GCC nationals**), **what it pays**, and **when to apply** for the 2027 intake. Scholarships that are generous but don't cover both are listed separately at the end, so you know the difference.

> **Last reviewed and eligibility checked:** 18 November 2026. Every scholarship can change its rules each cycle. Before you apply, confirm eligibility, amounts and deadlines on the official page linked for each entry.

## 1. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (European Union)

**What it is:** EU-funded master's programmes run by consortia of universities. You study in two or more European countries.

- **Pays:** your programme's participation costs, travel and installation costs, and a living allowance widely reported at **€1,400 per month** for the length of the programme (up to 24 months).
- **Who can apply:** students of **any nationality**. Non-EU applicants are eligible for the full scholarship.
- **When:** you apply **directly to each programme**. Most 2027 deadlines fall between **October 2026 and January 2027**.
- **Tip:** there are well over a hundred programmes. Apply to the ones that genuinely fit your degree, because consortia select on academic fit.

**Official:** [Erasmus Mundus catalogue](https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en)

## 2. Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (Sweden)

**What it is:** Sweden's flagship government scholarship for professionals from eligible countries, for master's study in Sweden.

- **Pays:** **tuition, living costs and insurance**.
- **Who can apply:** citizens of an eligible-country list, which **includes Pakistan**. GCC countries haven't been on recent lists. You'll need **work and leadership experience**.
- **When:** the application window is usually around **February**, after you've applied to your Swedish master's programme through University Admissions.

**Official:** [Swedish Institute: SI Scholarship for Global Professionals](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/)

## 3. Gates Cambridge Scholarship (UK)

**What it is:** one of the world's most prestigious scholarships, for full-time graduate study at the University of Cambridge.

- **Pays:** the **full cost of study**: university fees, a maintenance allowance of **£23,152 for 12 months (2026–27 rate)**, flights at the start and end of the course, and inbound visa costs including the health surcharge.
- **Who can apply:** **any nationality except UK**.
- **When (2027 entry):** you apply through the Cambridge graduate application by your course's funding deadline, **8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027**, depending on the course.

**Official:** [Gates Cambridge](https://www.gatescambridge.org)

## 4. Clarendon Fund (University of Oxford)

**What it is:** Oxford's largest graduate scholarship scheme, offering **more than 200 fully funded scholarships a year**.

- **Pays:** Oxford describes Clarendon scholarships as fully funded. Check the current coverage on Oxford's funding pages.
- **Who can apply:** graduate applicants of **all nationalities**. In most cases you're considered **automatically** when you apply for an eligible course by its January deadline.
- **When:** apply for your Oxford course by the **December or January** course deadline.

**Official:** [University of Oxford: Clarendon Fund](https://www.ox.ac.uk/clarendon)

## 5. Rhodes Scholarship (University of Oxford)

**What it is:** the world's oldest international scholarship programme, for graduate study at Oxford.

- **Pays:** Oxford fees and a living stipend for the length of the course.
- **Who can apply:** applicants from specific **constituencies**. **Pakistan** has its own constituency, with **two scholarships a year** for Pakistani citizens. The **UAE** has its own constituency, and students from countries without one can apply through the **Global** scholarships.
- **When:** deadlines vary by constituency, typically in the summer or early autumn of the year before entry.

**Official:** [Rhodes Trust: constituency information](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/constituency-information/)

## 6. Chevening Scholarships (UK)

**What it is:** the UK government's global scholarship for future leaders, for a one-year master's in the UK.

- **Pays:** **tuition**, a **monthly living allowance**, a **return flight**, the cost of one visa application and settling-in costs.
- **Who can apply:** citizens of **160+ Chevening-eligible countries and territories**, **including Pakistan**. Check the official list for your country. You'll usually need **two years of work experience**.
- **When:** the **2027/28 round closed on 6 October 2026**. The next round usually opens in **August**.

**Official:** [Chevening](https://www.chevening.org)

## 7. Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (UK)

**What it is:** UK government (FCDO-funded) scholarships for candidates from eligible low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries, focused on development impact.

- **Pays:** tuition, a living allowance, return airfare and other allowances.
- **Who can apply:** citizens and permanent residents of eligible Commonwealth countries. **Pakistan is eligible.** GCC states aren't Commonwealth members.
- **When:** the **2027/28 round ran from 8 September to 20 October 2026** and has closed. Pakistani applicants are also nominated through national bodies.

**Official:** [Commonwealth Scholarship Commission: Master's Scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarship/)

## 8. DAAD EPOS: Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (Germany)

**What it is:** DAAD scholarships for professionals from developing countries, for **pre-approved, development-related** master's and PhD courses at German universities.

- **Pays:** a monthly stipend reported at **€992 for master's students**, plus travel and health-insurance allowances. Tuition is usually free at German public universities, though a semester contribution may apply.
- **Who can apply:** graduates from eligible countries, **including Pakistan**, with **at least two years' relevant work experience**.
- **When:** most courses for **2027/28 closed around 15 October 2026**. You apply to up to **three** EPOS courses directly.

**Official:** [DAAD: EPOS scholarship database](https://www.daad.de/epos)

## 9. ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme (Switzerland)

**What it is:** ETH Zurich's merit scholarship for outstanding master's students.

- **Pays:** a **full tuition waiver** plus a grant of **CHF 12,000 per semester**, rising to **CHF 13,500 per semester from Autumn 2027**, for the regular length of the master's.
- **Who can apply:** excellent students of **all nationalities** who don't already hold a master's degree.
- **When:** the application window for 2027 entry is **1–30 November 2026**. It's open now, so check the official page today.

**Official:** [ETH Zurich: Excellence Scholarship & Opportunity Programme](https://ethz.ch/en/studies/financial/scholarships/excellencescholarship.html)

## 10. Stipendium Hungaricum (Hungary)

**What it is:** the Hungarian government's scholarship for students from partner countries, at **bachelor's, master's and PhD** level. It's one of the few on this list open to **undergraduates**.

- **Pays:** **tuition-free study**, a monthly stipend (reported at **HUF 43,700** for bachelor's and master's students, more for PhD), a **dormitory place or housing contribution**, and health insurance.
- **Who can apply:** citizens of partner countries. **Pakistan is a partner country.** Pakistani applicants also apply through **HEC Pakistan**.
- **When:** the main call usually closes in **mid-January**. For 2026/27, HEC's deadline was 15 January 2026.
- **Be realistic:** the stipend is modest, so budget for some living costs of your own.

**Official:** [Stipendium Hungaricum](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu) · [HEC Pakistan: Stipendium Hungaricum](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/SHSP/Pages/default.aspx)

## Generous, but not fully funded

These are excellent awards, but they don't cover both tuition and living costs, so we've kept them off the main list:

- **EPFL Excellence Fellowships (Switzerland):** **CHF 10,000 per semester** for master's students. Tuition is **not waived**.
- **Government of Ireland International Education Scholarships:** a **€10,000 stipend** for one year of postgraduate study. Reported fee coverage varies and eligibility rules have differed between sources, so check the official HEA call.

## How to give yourself the best chance

1. **Apply to the university and the scholarship.** Most of these need an offer, or an application to the programme, as well.
2. **Start 12 months early.** Many deadlines fall in **October to January**, almost a year before you'd start.
3. **Get your documents in order:** transcripts, degree certificate, English test, CV and references. [See the full document checklist →](/guides/university-application-document-checklist)
4. **Write for the scholarship's purpose.** Chevening wants future leaders, Commonwealth wants development impact, and Gates wants commitment to improving others' lives.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which scholarships in Europe are fully funded?

Scholarships that cover tuition and pay a living allowance include Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters, the Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon, Rhodes, Chevening, Commonwealth Master's Scholarships, DAAD EPOS, the ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship and Stipendium Hungaricum.

### Can Pakistani students get fully funded scholarships in Europe?

Yes. Pakistani citizens are eligible for all ten scholarships on this list, including Rhodes, which has a dedicated Pakistan constituency, and Stipendium Hungaricum, where Pakistani applicants also apply through HEC.

### Are there fully funded scholarships in Europe for students from the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Erasmus Mundus, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon and the ETH Zurich Excellence Scholarship are open to all nationalities, and the UAE has its own Rhodes constituency. Country-list programmes such as the Swedish Institute and Commonwealth scholarships generally don't include GCC states.

### Are there fully funded bachelor's scholarships in Europe?

They're rarer. Stipendium Hungaricum is the main fully funded government scheme on this list that covers bachelor's degrees. For low-cost alternatives, see our guide to [studying in Europe after A-levels](/guides/study-in-europe-after-a-levels).
