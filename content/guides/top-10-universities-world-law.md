# Top 10 Universities in the World for Law

Law is one of the most popular degrees among students from **Pakistan and the GCC**, and one of the most confusing to plan internationally, because **law is taught differently in different countries**. In the UK and Singapore you can study law straight after school. In the US, law is a graduate degree.

This guide lists the **top 10 universities for law** in the **QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026**, and explains which degree, an **LLB, JD or LLM**, gets you into each one.

> **Ranking used:** QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, Law & Legal Studies. Rankings reflect academic and employer reputation and research, not your chances of admission. **Last reviewed:** 18 November 2026.

## LLB, JD or LLM: which route fits you?

### Straight after A-levels: an undergraduate law degree (UK, Singapore)

**Oxford, Cambridge, LSE** and **NUS** offer **undergraduate law** you can apply to straight after A-levels or the IB.

- **UK:** you apply through **UCAS**. **Oxford and Cambridge close on 15 October** for the following year's entry. Several leading UK universities also require the **LNAT** admissions test.
- **Subjects:** law rarely requires specific A-levels, but essay-based subjects help. [A-level requirements by degree →](/guides/a-level-subjects-for-medicine-engineering-business-law)
- **For Pakistan:** a UK law degree is a popular route for students who plan to qualify through the **Bar**. Check the current professional requirements in the jurisdiction where you plan to practise.

### After a bachelor's degree: the JD (US)

All six US universities in the top 10 teach law as the **JD**, a graduate degree taken **after a bachelor's degree**, usually with the **LSAT** or another accepted test. A JD is mainly for students who intend to practise **in the US**.

### After a law degree: the LLM

Every university on this list offers an **LLM**, a one-year master's for students who **already hold a law degree**. It's the most common way international lawyers from **Pakistan and the GCC** study at Harvard, Yale, Columbia, NYU or Berkeley.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which university is best for law in the world?

Harvard University is first for law in the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, followed by Oxford, Cambridge, Yale and Stanford.

### Can I study law straight after A-levels?

Yes, in the UK and Singapore. Oxford, Cambridge, LSE and NUS all offer undergraduate law. In the US, law (the JD) is a graduate degree taken after a bachelor's.

### What's the difference between an LLB, a JD and an LLM?

An LLB is an undergraduate law degree, as in the UK. A JD is the US professional law degree, taken after a bachelor's. An LLM is a postgraduate law master's for people who already have a law degree.

### Do I need specific A-levels for law?

Usually not. Most universities set no required subjects for law, but several leading UK universities require the LNAT, and strong essay-writing subjects help.
