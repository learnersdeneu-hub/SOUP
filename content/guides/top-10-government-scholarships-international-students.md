# Top 10 Government Scholarships for International Students

Governments fund some of the most generous scholarships in the world, because they're investing in long-term relationships with future leaders. These programmes usually cover **tuition, living costs, travel and insurance**, and several of them are open to **undergraduates** as well as postgraduates.

This list covers ten major government programmes that international students, including **Pakistani and GCC nationals**, can apply to. It sets out what each one covers and how the application route works, because many government scholarships are applied for **through your home country's embassy or education ministry**, not directly with a university.

> **Last reviewed and eligibility checked:** 18 November 2026. Government scholarship rules, quotas and deadlines change every year. Always confirm on the official page and, where it applies, with the scholarship office in your country.

## 1. Fulbright Foreign Student Program (United States)

- **Covers:** tuition, required textbooks, airfare, a living stipend and health insurance.
- **Levels:** master's and PhD in all disciplines **except clinical medicine**.
- **Pakistan:** run by the **United States Educational Foundation in Pakistan (USEFP)**. Master's applicants need **16 years of education**, and PhD applicants **18 years**. The deadline for 2027 study was **1 April 2026**, so expect the next cycle around spring 2027.
- **GCC:** Fulbright operates through US missions and commissions, and availability varies by country, so check your local programme.

**Official:** [USEFP: Fulbright degree programs](https://usefp.org/scholarships/fulbright-degree.cfm)

## 2. Chevening (United Kingdom)

The UK government's flagship scholarship for a **one-year master's**. It covers tuition, a living allowance, flights and visa costs. The **2027/28 round closed on 6 October 2026**. [Full details in our Europe scholarships guide →](/guides/top-10-fully-funded-scholarships-europe)

**Official:** [Chevening](https://www.chevening.org)

## 3. Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (United Kingdom)

FCDO-funded master's scholarships for citizens of eligible low- and middle-income **Commonwealth** countries, **including Pakistan**. The 2027/28 round closed on **20 October 2026**.

**Official:** [Commonwealth Scholarship Commission](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarship/)

## 4. DAAD scholarships (Germany)

Germany's academic exchange service funds study and research across many programmes. For professionals from developing countries, **EPOS** funds pre-approved, development-related master's and PhD courses, with a stipend reported at **€992 per month** for master's students. [EPOS details →](/guides/top-10-fully-funded-scholarships-europe)

**Official:** [DAAD scholarship database](https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/)

## 5. MEXT Scholarship (Japan)

- **Covers:** tuition, a monthly allowance and travel.
- **Levels:** **undergraduate**, research student (leading to a master's or PhD), and other categories.
- **Route:** most applicants apply through the **Embassy of Japan** in their country of nationality. A UAE national applies through the embassy in the UAE, and a **Pakistani national living in the UAE** generally applies through **Pakistan**.
- **Timing:** embassy deadlines for the 2027 intake were in **May 2026**, followed by written exams. The next cycle typically opens in **spring**.

**Official:** [Study in Japan: MEXT embassy recommendation](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/smap-stopj-applications-research.html) · [Embassy of Japan in Pakistan](https://www.pk.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/MEXT_Research_Scholarship.html) · [Embassy of Japan in the UAE](https://www.uae.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/r_mext2027.html)

## 6. Global Korea Scholarship (South Korea)

- **Covers:** tuition, a monthly allowance, airfare, Korean language training and insurance.
- **Levels:** undergraduate (GKS-U) and graduate (GKS-G).
- **Route:** through the **Korean Embassy** or directly through a **designated Korean university**.
- **Timing:** the **undergraduate** embassy track for 2027 has opened. **Graduate** guidelines are usually published in **early spring**.

**Official:** [Study in Korea (NIIED)](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr)

## 7. Chinese Government Scholarship (China)

- **Covers (full scholarship):** tuition, on-campus accommodation, medical insurance and a monthly stipend. Official provincial and university pages list **CNY 3,000 per month** for master's students and **CNY 3,500** for PhD students.
- **Levels:** undergraduate, master's and PhD, often with a Chinese-language preparatory year for Chinese-taught programmes.
- **Route:** through a Chinese university (Type B) or the Chinese embassy (Type A). Deadlines usually fall between **January and April**.

**Official:** [Campus China (CSC application system)](https://www.campuschina.org)

## 8. Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye)

- **Covers:** tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend, health insurance, return airfare and a year of Turkish-language training.
- **Levels:** undergraduate, master's and PhD.
- **Who:** open to citizens of most countries.
- **Timing:** the main window has usually run from **January to February**. Watch for the official 2027 announcement.

**Official:** [Türkiye Scholarships](https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr)

## 9. Stipendium Hungaricum (Hungary)

Tuition-free study, a monthly stipend, housing support and insurance, at **bachelor's, master's and PhD** level, for citizens of partner countries, **including Pakistan** (through HEC). [Details in our Europe guide →](/guides/top-10-fully-funded-scholarships-europe)

**Official:** [Stipendium Hungaricum](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu)

## 10. Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (Sweden)

Covers tuition, living costs and insurance for a master's in Sweden, for professionals from eligible countries, **including Pakistan**. [Details in our Europe guide →](/guides/top-10-fully-funded-scholarships-europe)

**Official:** [Swedish Institute](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/)

## Government scholarships for GCC nationals

Students from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman are usually **not** eligible for development-focused schemes such as Commonwealth, EPOS and the Swedish Institute. But many students from these countries can apply for:

- their **own government's sponsorship** programmes. Check the sponsor's **approved universities list** before you apply, because these lists change.
- **MEXT, GKS, CSC and Türkiye Bursları**, through the relevant embassy.
- **Chevening**, if your country is on the current eligibility list.
- university-funded awards open to all nationalities, such as **Gates Cambridge, Clarendon and ETH Zurich**. [See the Europe list →](/guides/top-10-fully-funded-scholarships-europe)

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best government scholarships for international students?

Major government programmes include Fulbright (US), Chevening and Commonwealth (UK), DAAD (Germany), MEXT (Japan), the Global Korea Scholarship, the Chinese Government Scholarship, Türkiye Bursları, Stipendium Hungaricum and the Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals.

### Which government scholarships are open to undergraduates?

MEXT, the Global Korea Scholarship, the Chinese Government Scholarship, Türkiye Bursları and Stipendium Hungaricum all offer undergraduate scholarships. Fulbright, Chevening and Commonwealth are for postgraduates.

### Do Pakistani students apply through HEC or the embassy?

It depends on the programme. Fulbright applications go through USEFP, Stipendium Hungaricum through HEC and the online portal, MEXT and GKS usually through the relevant embassy, and Chevening directly online.

### Are government scholarships fully funded?

The programmes on this list generally cover tuition, a living allowance and travel, though stipend levels vary widely. Check what each programme actually pays for your city and level.
