> **Draft:** places 5–9 must be verified on the official ShanghaiRanking page before publication. See the review notes.

Aerospace engineering is one of the few subjects where research rankings look very different from general university rankings. In **ShanghaiRanking's 2025 subject ranking for Aerospace Engineering**, which measures research output and impact, Chinese universities with long histories in aviation and space research hold the top four places.
